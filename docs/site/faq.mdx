<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is NYXERON?">
    A nightclub game on NEAR. A host opens a party and sets the drink prices. Guests buy drinks, and every drink holds
    0.001 of a real share that the guest keeps.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is it real money?">
    Not yet. The live game runs on NEAR testnet, with test dollars you can get once a day. The mainnet purchase of real
    shares is built and waits for its first run.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a wallet?">
    No. Sign in with Google or email and you get your own NEAR account. There is no seed phrase, no wallet pop-up and no
    gas.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What do I own?">
    For every drink, 0.001 of a real share, held as NEP-245 units in your own NEAR account. Three Tesla drinks are 0.003
    of a Tesla share.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do the shares come from?">
    They are Robinhood stock tokens on Robinhood Chain. They are bought by a warehouse address that a NEAR account
    controls through Chain Signatures. [Real shares and Proof](/real-shares) explains the purchase and how to check it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When do my shares arrive?">
    About 90 seconds after the party ends. A House Party on testnet lasts 10 minutes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the game take my dollars?">
    No. Only you can sign a payment from your account. Our relayer pays the gas for three kinds of action you signed: a
    drink payment to the contract, your own cash-out and a host's margin claim. It refuses everything else. If a price
    is outside the host's allowed range, the contract refunds the whole payment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get my money out?">
    **Cash out** sends your dollars to almost any chain through NEAR Intents. **Add money** brings them in the same way.
    On testnet both show real quotes and send nothing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I try it without an account?">
    Yes. Practice runs a full night with play money and live stock prices, with no sign-in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I host a party?">
    Yes. In City, tap **Switch to CEO**, then **Host a party**. You set each drink's price between wholesale and 3×
    wholesale, and you keep the margin on every drink sold.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is it open source?">Yes, under the MIT license.</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
