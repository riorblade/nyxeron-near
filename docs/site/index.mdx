NYXERON is a nightclub game. A host (the CEO) opens a party and sets the drink prices. Guests buy drinks, and every drink holds **0.001 of a real share**: Tesla, NVIDIA, Apple, the S&P 500 and more. When the party ends, the guest owns those shares in their own NEAR account.

## Why

Owning a stock takes a brokerage account. Owning one on chain takes a wallet, a seed phrase, gas and a bridge. Neither feels like play.

NYXERON hides all of that behind a game. A player signs in with Google or email, sees a balance in dollars and taps **Buy** on a drink. There is no wallet pop-up, no seed phrase and no gas. A few minutes later they hold a slice of a real share, without having to learn what a blockchain is.

## What is live

The game runs on **NEAR testnet** at [app.nyxeron.xyz](https://app.nyxeron.xyz), with test dollars you can get once a day. Real NEAR transactions carry every step: the payments, the contract's split, the release, the mint and the delivery. The only step that is simulated on testnet is buying the stock tokens themselves; [Real shares and Proof](/real-shares) explains why.

## Three ways in

|              | What it is                                                                                      | Account         |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| **Practice** | Run a club called The Basement for a night, with play money and live stock prices.              | None            |
| **City**     | Real parties on testnet. Host one as CEO, or go out as a guest and buy drinks.                  | Google or email |
| **Proof**    | The contract, the stocks behind the units, and every party's steps, with links to the explorer. | None            |

## Built on NEAR

| Part              | NEAR feature                                                                                                |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sign-in           | Google or email gives each player a NEAR account of their own, through Privy                                |
| No gas            | NEP-366 meta-transactions: the player signs, our relayer pays the gas                                       |
| Paying for drinks | USDC sent to our contract with a price quote the server signed; the contract checks it and splits the money |
| Owning shares     | Stock units as NEP-245 multi tokens, 1 unit = 0.001 share                                                   |
| Real stocks       | Robinhood stock tokens, bought by an address a NEAR account controls through Chain Signatures               |
| Money in and out  | NEAR Intents (1Click), from and to almost any chain                                                         |
