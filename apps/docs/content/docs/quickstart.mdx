Open [nyxeron.xyz](https://nyxeron.xyz) and tap **Launch app**, or go straight to [app.nyxeron.xyz](https://app.nyxeron.xyz). Steps 1 to 4 need no account.

## Practice, no sign-in

<Steps>
  <Step>

    ### Open Practice

    Tap **Practice**, then **Play** on the title screen.

    **You see** your 3D club, The Basement, and a menu of real stocks at live market prices.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Set the night up

    In the dock, open **Menu** and move a drink's price slider. Try **DJ**. Then tap **Open club** at the bottom right.

    **You see** guests walk in and buy at the bar, with the margin of each sale floating over them.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Run the night

    Try **5×** or **20×** speed and **Happy hour**, then **Skip**.

    **You see** the night report: margin, reputation, goals, why it went this way, and a receipt of what sold.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Test a week

    Tap **Next night**, then **Week** in the dock.

    **You see** seven nights simulated at once, with each night's guests, drinks, margin and reputation.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## City, on testnet

<Steps>
  <Step>

    ### Sign in

    Go back to the start, tap **City**, pick **Go out as a guest** and sign in with Google or email.

    **You see** a NEAR account made for you, shown in the bar with a link to the explorer, and a balance in dollars.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Get test dollars

    Tap **Get test dollars**.

    **You see** "$3.00 on the way." and the balance goes to $3.00. This is test USDC in your own account, once a day.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Buy a drink

    Open **House Party** (always on, top of the list) and tap **×3** next to TSLA.

    **You see** "3 × TSLA $… · paid" with a receipt link to the transaction. There is no pop-up and no gas.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Walk in the club (optional)

    On the party page tap **Walk in the club**. Walk with WASD or the arrows, or the touch stick on a phone.

    **You see** your own avatar among the crowd, with three cameras and dances.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Collect your shares

    A House Party lasts 10 minutes. When it ends, look at **Collect shares** in your portfolio.

    **You see** "Delivering your shares…", then "Delivered to your portfolio." with a delivery receipt, and "TSLA 0.003 share" under **Shares you hold**.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Add money

    Tap **Add money**, keep USDC on Base, type any address for the refund, and tap **Get a quote**.

    **You see** a live NEAR Intents quote, such as "You send 10.0 USDC on Base. You get about $9.99… here". On testnet nothing is sent.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Host your own party

    Tap **Switch to CEO**, then **Host a party**. Give it a name and a length, and tap **Open it**.

    **You see** your party page with **Your prices**. Move a slider and tap **Save prices**, anywhere from wholesale to 3× wholesale. You keep the margin.

  </Step>
  <Step>

    ### Check it on chain

    Open [app.nyxeron.xyz/proof](https://app.nyxeron.xyz/proof) and tap a party under **Party by party** where drinks were bought.

    **You see** the contract, USDC and price key on NEAR, the units against the shares behind them, and each party's release, mint and settle with transaction links.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout>
  The test dollars, the House Party and the dry-run quotes for Add money and Cash out exist on testnet only.
</Callout>
